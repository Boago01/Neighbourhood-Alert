Safety Resources

Practical advice to help you protect yourself, your family and your community.

Stay alert and stay safe
If you are in immediate danger, move to a safe location and contact the appropriate emergency services. Do not put yourself at risk to investigate suspicious activity.

Safety Tips and Guidelines

Crime Prevention

Simple habits can help reduce your risk of becoming a victim of crime.

  • Keep doors, windows and gates locked.
  • Keep valuables out of sight.
  • Stay alert when walking in public.
  • Avoid displaying large amounts of cash.
  • Report damaged locks or security lights.
Reporting Suspicious Activity

Help your community by reporting concerns responsibly.

  • Use Neighbourhood Alert to report incidents.
  • Provide accurate descriptions and locations.
  • Separate what you observed from assumptions.
  • Never confront a potentially dangerous person.
  • Avoid spreading unverified accusations online.
Personal Safety

Take precautions when travelling or spending time outside your home.

  • Plan your route before travelling.
  • Stay in well-lit, populated areas where possible.
  • Tell someone you trust about your travel plans.
  • Keep your phone charged when travelling.
  • Trust your instincts and move to safety if concerned.
Home and Neighbourhood Safety

Work with trusted neighbours to improve community safety.

  • Check that doors and windows are secured.
  • Keep entrances and pathways well lit.
  • Do not share access codes with strangers.
  • Look out for vulnerable neighbours when safe.
  • Report hazards and security concerns promptly.
Digital and Personal Information Safety

Protect your identity and personal information when using websites and social media.

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Never share passwords or verification codes.
  • Be careful with suspicious links and messages.
  • Avoid posting your live location publicly.
  • Do not publish another person's private details.
Staying Safe During Emergencies

Knowing what to do can help you respond more safely during an emergency.

  • Move away from immediate danger if possible.
  • Contact the appropriate emergency services.
  • Give your location and clear information.
  • Follow instructions from emergency responders.
  • Do not enter dangerous areas to collect evidence.
